Developing Teaching & Learning Practice

My contribution to modern teaching is centred on connecting sound pedagogy with practical innovation: designing resources, using evidence from assessment, and building digital tools that reduce routine work and create more space for meaningful teaching.

Plan → Teach → Assess → Reflect

I treat teaching as an iterative process. Clear objectives guide instruction; classroom interaction and assessment provide evidence; feedback and reflection then inform adaptation, reteaching and future planning.

Technology with Pedagogical Purpose

I integrate technology where it adds educational value: organizing resources, creating interactive materials, supporting differentiated practice, improving access to content and helping learners become more independent.

Resource & Assessment Design

I develop lesson materials, worksheets, diagnostic activities, tests and rubrics around specific learning objectives. This supports clearer expectations, more useful feedback and better-informed instructional decisions.

Building Tools for Education

My interest in web development extends into education. I explore how lightweight digital systems can help organize lesson planning, classroom resources and recurring teacher workflows while keeping pedagogy at the centre.
